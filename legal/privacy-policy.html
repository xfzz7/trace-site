迹绎隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日
适用范围：迹绎（Trace）iOS 应用

迹绎不收集你的任何数据。照片、位置、轨迹全部在你的手机上处理，不上传、不共享、不出设备。

我们收集的信息

不收集。迹绎没有账号体系，没有服务端，不含任何第三方 SDK、分析工具或广告组件，也不会向开发者自有服务器发送任何网络请求。

你在 App 里产生的全部内容——选中的照片与视频、绘制出的轨迹、地名、旅程统计与历史记录——只保存在你的设备本机（系统偏好设置存储），卸载 App 即完整清除。

我们申请的权限

权限用途是否必需
读取照片读取你主动选择的照片与视频的拍摄时间、地理位置元数据，用于在地图上还原轨迹。只看内置示例时不会申请这项权限使用自己的照片时需要
写入照片仅在你主动使用「补充位置」功能并选择保存时，把补上的拍摄地点写回该照片的元数据可选，按需触发

你可以随时在系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」中调整或撤销这些权限。撤销读取权限后，App 将无法读取新的照片素材。

关于位置信息

迹绎不使用你的设备定位服务。我们不会请求定位权限，也不会获取你当前所在的位置。

App 使用的位置信息，是照片文件中已内嵌的 EXIF GPS 元数据——它由你的相机在拍摄时写入，随照片一起存在你的相册里。我们读取它，仅用于在地图上绘制轨迹，以及在你主动操作时把位置补写回照片。内置示例旅程使用随应用分发的坐标，不读取你的位置。

Apple 系统服务

迹绎使用 Apple 提供的两项系统能力，它们会与 Apple 的服务器通信：

  • 地图显示（MapKit）：加载地图底图与地形数据。
  • 地名反查（CLGeocoder）：把经纬度转换成可读的地名。

这两项服务由 Apple 运营，遵循 Apple 的隐私政策。除此之外，迹绎不发起任何其他网络请求。

数据保留与删除

所有数据仅存在于你的设备本机。你可以在 App 内左滑删除单条旅程记录，或卸载 App 以彻底清除全部数据。由于我们不持有你的任何数据，因此不存在需要我们配合删除的服务器端副本。

儿童隐私

迹绎适合所有年龄段的用户使用。我们不收集任何人的数据。

不包含的内容

迹绎不包含：用户账号与注册登录、内购与付费、广告、用户生成内容的上传与分享、社交功能、数据分析与行为追踪、推送通知。

政策变更

如本政策发生实质性变更，我们会更新本页顶部的生效日期，并在 App 新版本的说明中提示。

Trace Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
Scope: Trace (迹绎) for iOS

Trace collects no data. Your photos, locations and routes are processed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere.

Information we collect

None. Trace has no accounts, no server backend, and contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, or advertising components. It sends no network requests to any developer-owned server.

Everything you create in the app — selected photos and videos, generated routes, place names, trip statistics and history — is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Permissions we request

PermissionPurposeRequired
Read Photo LibraryRead the capture time and location metadata of photos and videos you choose, to reconstruct your route on a map. The built-in sample does not ask for this permissionRequired when you choose your own photos
Add to Photo LibraryOnly when you use “Add Location” and choose to save: write the added location back into that photo’s metadataOptional, on demand

You can change or revoke these at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Location

Trace does not use your device’s location services. We do not request location permission and never obtain your current position.

The location data the app uses is the EXIF GPS metadata already embedded in your photo files, written by your camera at capture time. We read it only to draw routes on a map, and to write a location back into a photo when you explicitly ask us to. The built-in sample voyage uses coordinates shipped with the app and does not read your location.

Apple system services

Trace uses two Apple-provided system capabilities that communicate with Apple’s servers:

  • Map display (MapKit) — loading basemap and terrain data.
  • Reverse geocoding (CLGeocoder) — converting coordinates into readable place names.

Both are operated by Apple and governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Beyond these, the app makes no other network requests.

Retention and deletion

All data exists only on your device. You can swipe to delete an individual trip in the app, or uninstall the app to erase everything. Since we hold no data of yours, there is no server-side copy for us to delete.

Children’s privacy

Trace is suitable for all ages. We collect no data from anyone.

What the app does not contain

No accounts or sign-in, no in-app purchases, no advertising, no uploading or sharing of user-generated content, no social features, no analytics or tracking, no push notifications.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and note it in the release notes of a new version.
