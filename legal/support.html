迹绎技术支持

迹绎（Trace）是一个装在你手机上的照片轨迹回放工具。没有账号，没有广告。照片和轨迹不出设备。

联系邮箱：whozz7@qq.com

先看一段示例

打开 App，如果还没有自己的旅程，屏幕中央有「看看示例」。点一下就会播放一段内置旅程：上海外滩、豫园、西湖断桥、灵隐寺、拙政园。

这段示例不读取你的相册，也不会写进你的历史记录。

播放自己的照片

  1. 点地图右上角的 ＋。
  2. 允许访问照片。要看全部可选的照片，请选「允许访问全部照片」。
  3. 选一批带拍摄位置的照片，点「开启我的迹绎」。
  4. 播放时可以暂停、左右滑动，或点顶部的页数胶囊拖动进度。
  5. 播完后，底部会留下这次的距离、天数和经过的地方。回到首页可以再看，左滑可以删除这一条。

手机自己拍的照片通常带有位置。截图、以及从微信等应用保存的图片，通常没有。

照片没有位置时

选完后会进入「补充位置」。可以搜索地点，或在地图上拖动图钉。确认之后，可以选择把这个位置写回照片本身。不写回也可以直接播放。

迹绎不使用你手机的定位。它用的是照片里已经有的位置，或你刚刚手动标上的位置。

常见问题

点了 ＋ 没有反应，或提示无法访问照片。
到系统「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片 → 迹绎」，改成允许访问。

播放时地名一直在转，或地图是灰的。
地图和地名来自 Apple 的系统服务，需要联网。照片本身不会因此被上传。

删掉一条旅程后，照片还在相册里吗？
在。迹绎只删自己的记录，不删相册里的照片。

示例旅程会出现在首页列表里吗？
不会。

Trace Support

Trace replays a set of photos on a map, in the order they were taken. There is no account and no advertising. Photos and routes stay on your iPhone.

Contact: whozz7@qq.com

Watch a sample first

On an empty home screen, tap “See a sample”. It plays a built-in trip down the California coast: the Golden Gate Bridge, Lombard Street, Bixby Creek Bridge, McWay Falls, and Santa Monica Pier. (With the device language set to Chinese, the sample is a trip through Shanghai, Hangzhou and Suzhou instead.)

The sample does not read your photo library and is not saved to your history.

Play your own photos

  1. Tap ＋ at the top right of the map.
  2. Allow photo access. Choose “Allow Full Access” if you want the whole library available.
  3. Pick photos that have a location, then tap “Start My Voyage”.
  4. During playback you can pause, swipe between photos, or drag the progress pill at the top.
  5. At the end you get the distance, the number of days, and the places. Back on the home screen, swipe a row left to delete that voyage.

Photos taken on an iPhone usually include a location. Screenshots and images saved from other apps usually do not.

When a photo has no location

The app opens “Add location”. Search for a place, or drag the pin. You can write that location back into the photo, or skip the write and just play.

Trace does not use your device’s location services. It uses the location already stored in the photo, or the one you just placed.

Common questions

Nothing happens when I tap ＋, or the app says it cannot read photos.
Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Trace, and allow access.

Place names keep spinning, or the map stays gray.
Maps and place names come from Apple’s system services and need a network connection. Your photos are not uploaded.

If I delete a voyage, are the photos deleted from my library?
No. Only the voyage record inside Trace is removed.

Does the sample show up in my list?
No.
